PORTFOLIO GUIDE TEMPLATE

Cobalt Phial

A tool utilised to sample the divine energy trapped within certain creatures.

Siphon

Regain lost health points rather than dealing damage upon using this tool on other entities

Bonk

Launch the targeted entity back by a moderate distance

Withering Bash

A projectile-based weapon that will shoot Wither Skulls at the cost of consuming Wither Skeleton Skulls.

Viral

Transforms shot Wither Skeleton Skull projectiles into their explosive form at the cost of range

Momentum

Increases Wither Skeleton Skull projectile speed and decreases cooldown after use

Suit

Increases power of shot Wither Skeleton Skull projectiles at the cost of additional ammo being consumed per shot

Flask of Desolatic Burst

A consumable item that releases a burst of ender energy to damage any entities within in - excluding you!

Enlarge

Increases the radius of the burst created

Final Stand

If this item is held in the offhand, create a burst of desolatic energy when reaching 1 health rather than upon consumption

Hazardous

Poisons entities that walk through the burst

Arsonist

Lights entities ablaze that walk through the burst

Enforced Chestplate (Gear)

A chestplate forged from the divine energy of a brutish swine, comparable armour points to that of Netherite.

Note: Technically this mostly applies to any enforced gear, but currently only a chestplate exists for the armour set

Gilded Skin

Provides resistence 3 if the remaining pieces of armour equipped are golden, but every item takes more durability damage

Brute

Gain a movement speed increase and a great amount of protection from incoming damage

Bulwart

Hold crouch for a small duration to release a cloud of fungal fumes, gaining Resistance V while active

Shadowkin Growth

A pair of boots that disallow any and all vibrations from being detected from the player

Hivemind

Wardens cannot target the wearer

Infest

Slows down other entities within a radius if the wearer and the target is standing on Sculk blocks

Catatonic

Makes the wearer significantly weaker while not standing on Sculk blocks, but grants a speed and damage boost otherwise

Prismatic Shuffler

A tool that upon use, cleanses the player of Mining Fatigue for a small duration of time

Note: This functionality disappears when the Sharptooth enchantment is applied

Sharptooth

Turns the Prismatic Shuffler into a weapon rather than a utility item

Expire

Makes the user able to suffocate above water, but allows them to freely breath underwater

Refreshing

Grants Regeneration II while fully submerged underwater while in-hand
