Quack Quack Quack!

For the first time, Carl Mod and a couple of other projects have received an update dedicated to joke content. For everyone who played around with these joke versions, hope you had fun discovering the weird and quirky features added or adjusted for the update!

Here is a culmination of all of the stuff from Carl Mod's Quackiness update!

Easter Eggs Come To Life

Carl The Laying Space Duck

A few easter egg variants for Carl The Space Duck have managed to squirm their way into being proper mobs. One of these variants is Carl The Laying Space Duck who, you guessed it, lays eggs! However, unlike their less quacky relative, they lay a special kind of eggs that when thrown and make an impact with the ground, have a chance to spawn a baby version of Carl The Space Duck.

This specific variant spawns in the following biomes:

  • Plains
  • Sunflower Plains

Carl The Untitled Space Duck

A hostile variant of Carl emerges from the shadows! This variant spawns in the following biomes:

  • Forest
  • Flower Forest
  • Birch Forest
  • Old Growth Birch Forest
  • Dark Forest
  • Grove

Carl The Biblically Accurate Space Duck

A (more or less) mini-boss variant of Carl that can be summoned by feeding Carl The Space Duck a Nether Star. Once fed, a short time will need to pass up until the conversion is complete and the player can then deal damage to the divine being. Upon defeating it, the Nether Star will be dropped, returning the item to the player.

Carl The Space Duck (Changes)

While Carl himself isn't a new addition to the mod, clearly, there were some adjustments made to how he behaves! To start off, you can now find Carl The Space Duck spawning within Snowy Plains biomes, meaning you no longer have to go through the effort of finding an Igloo that happens to have a basement in order to find the adorable little ducky.

You can now feed seeds to 2 Carl The Space Ducks together and have them breed to make a baby Carl.

Evil Dakota be like: Let Carl be bred to make babies >:3

Carl Structures

Scattered throughout the End Dimension, floating embodiments of Carl and other select variants can be found. Each individual embodiment of the given variant have their own unique blockset tied to them. They probably have very limited use in actual building outside of recreating Carl several times over near your base, but I'm interested to see if anyone comes up with another use for these goofy blocks.

Big Carl

The unique blocks forming this structure are:

  • Block of Carl's Feathers
  • Carl's Beak Block
  • Helmet Shell Block
  • Helmet Lining Block

Big Untitled Carl

The unique blocks forming this structure are:

  • Block of Untitled Carl's Feathers
  • Untitled Carl's Beak Block

Big Biblically Accurate Carl

The unique blocks forming this structure are:

  • Block of Biblically Accurate Carl's Feathers
  • Carl's Beak Block
  • Angelic Ring Block
