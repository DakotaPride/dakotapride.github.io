Hello there! I am a freelance texture artist and mod developer. I have been creating pixel art and developing mods for Minecraft Java Edition for almost 6 years as of April 2026.

Below is the various textures and models that I've created over this time, ranging from blocks, items, creatures, and a plethora of skins to behold (including Figura)!
