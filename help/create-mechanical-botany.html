Compost Block

Content

A mod that adds some sub-sections of automation in regards for your crops, plants, and vegetation.

Functionality

Item & Block Interactions

Blocks of Compost, crafted from 9 Compost items within a 3x3 crafting grid, can be used as an alternative to tilled farmland. This block does not require water nearby in order for plants to thrive and does not need to be tilled to place plants atop of it.

Non-Content Compatibility
