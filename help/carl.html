Obtaining

Hidden in Igloos that happen to have a basement, a bucket with Carl inside can be found! Alongside our little duck friend, a yellow Smithing Template can be found to be used later.

Functionality

Carl doesn't really have much of a proper use aside from being cute - and let's be honest, that's plenty enough to expect from him.

He can be emptied from his bucket like most other "Mob in a Bucket" items, similar to Salmon or Cod.

But, oh no! Your friend has perished and dropped a plush form of himself. Well, from here, there's two things to do with him - either annoy your friends by right-clicking the cutie or use the previously mentioned Smithing Template in combination with both the Carl item and a Water Bucket within a Smithing Table to return him into his bucket.

The Smithing Template used in this process can be duplicated similar to other Smithing Templates, using 7 gold ingots and a snow block in the centre alongside the base template at the top-centre slot.

Easter Eggs

There are various easter eggs for Carl, specifically in regards to naming schemes! All of the names for each respective Carl variant is NOT case-sensitive, meaning lowercase and uppercase should work for each letter/character in the name.

Certain variants can be granted with the use of multiple different names, i.e., both barebones and bare_bones work just the same! For these cases, in the gallery below, the names are separated through commas.
